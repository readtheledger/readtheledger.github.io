What happens on the day

Scheduled rate announcements are at 9:45 a.m. Eastern. January, April, July and October decisions include a Monetary Policy Report. Check the Bank’s events page for press conferences; non-MPR decisions can also have one.

September 16, 2026 at 1:30 p.m. Eastern is the summary of deliberations for the September 2 decision, not a new decision. October 28 is the next scheduled rate announcement.
